Lead pipeline

The CRM flow is simple, visible, and repeatable.

Each stage is designed to answer one operational question: who is the customer, what do they need, how urgent is it, and what must happen next?

1

Quote captured

Service, package, home size, contact details, urgency, and notes are collected.

2

Lead enriched

The app adds score, tags, custom fields, total estimate range, and next actions.

3

Coordinator qualifies

The first reply confirms safety, equipment condition, address, access, and booking window.

4

Work scheduled

Install, repair, or maintenance work gets scheduled with the estimate assumptions attached.

5

Outcome logged

Job result, photos, invoice notes, and next reminder are recorded in the CRM.
